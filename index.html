How this estimate works

Past service
Service before 16 November 1995 uses a wage-slab multiplier and the Table B factor.

Formula pension
Service from 16 November 1995 is split at 31 August 2014 and 01 September 2014, with NCP deducted separately.

Scope
This version follows the workbook's member-pension logic and does not cover early, deferred, higher-wage or family pension options.
