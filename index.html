WORKBOOK NOTES

Read Me

This tool calculates a member's own EPS-95 monthly pension on normal age-58 superannuation, plus arrears from the date of commencement.

Core structure: Total monthly pension = Past Service Benefit for service before 16-11-1995 + Formula Pension for service from 16-11-1995, subject to the ₹1,000 statutory minimum.

Not covered: early pension, deferred pension, higher-wage pension option, and family / widow / children / orphan pension.

Service ledger: Each employment spell is split into past service, service up to 31-08-2014, and service from 01-09-2014. NCP days are deducted separately for the two periods.

Arrears: Full monthly pension is applied to completed months, with a pro-rated amount for the commencement month.
